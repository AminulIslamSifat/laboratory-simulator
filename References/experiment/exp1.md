Experiment Name: Study of Nameplate Ratings and Rated Speed (RPM) of
Electrical Machines
Objective:
This experiment aimed to examine the nameplate data of various electrical machines in
the Electrical Machines Laboratory. Key rated parameters, including voltage, current,
power, frequency, and rotational speed (RPM), were recorded for each machine. The
collected data were subsequently analyzed and compared to gain insight into the ratings
and operating characteristics of the different machines.
Required Apparatus:
1. DC Motor/Generator Set
2. Three-Phase Synchronous Motor/Generator
3. Single-Phase Transformer
4. Three-Phase Induction Motor
5. Variable Load Resistor Bank (Rheostat)
6. Fixed Load Resistors
7. Eddy-Current Dynamometer
8. Connecting leads (banana plug wires)
Theory:
Every electrical machine carries a nameplate that lists its important rated values,
including voltage, current, power, frequency, and rated speed in RPM. Rotating
machines such as DC, synchronous, and induction motors have different rated speeds
depending on their principle of operation. In contrast, non-rotating devices such as
transformers and load resistors are specified only by their electrical ratings. To apply
load to a machine and measure its performance during operation, an eddy-current
dynamometer is used.

Result:
The nameplate data of all the laboratory equipment were recorded successfully. The
rated speeds of the rotating machines are:
• DC Motor – 2500 RPM
• DC Generator – 3000 RPM
• Synchronous Motor/Generator – 3000 RPM
• Three-Phase Induction Motor – 2850 RPM
• The transformer and load resistors are static devices, so they do not have any rated
speed.
Discussion:
The nameplate values recorded were found to be consistent with the expected ratings of
the respective machines. The synchronous machine operates at a rated speed of 3000
RPM, whereas the induction motor runs slightly slower, at 2850 RPM, due to slip. The
DC motor and generator exhibit different rated speeds depending on their mode of
operation. The eddy-current dynamometer, meanwhile, is capable of operating at speeds
up to 4000 RPM.
Precautions:
• Do not exceed the rated voltage or current.
• Ensure all connections are correct before switching on the supply.
• Do not touch rotating parts while the machine is running.
• Record the nameplate data with the power switched off.
